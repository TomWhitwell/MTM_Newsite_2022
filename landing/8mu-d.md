  <section id="details" class="lp-minstore__details">
    <div>
      <h2>details</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>8 faders send midi cc</li>
        <li>4 buttons send notes or controllers</li>
        <li>8 banks stored on the device</li>
        <li>accelerometer sends lift / tilt / rotate / invert</li>
        <li>usb-c power and midi</li>
        <li>3.5mm trs midi, type a or type b</li>
        <li>browser editor for settings</li>
        <li>open-source firmware</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h2>not details</h2>
      <p>This page direction is intentionally spare. No big emotional arc, no long story. It relies on the object, a tight description and a clear details block. Quite dangerous if the photos are not excellent. Very good if they are.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="lp-minstore__details">
    <div>
      <h2>measurements</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>81 x 47 mm body</li>
        <li>13 mm body height</li>
        <li>24 mm with faders and caps</li>
        <li>about 65 g</li>
        <li>smaller than a credit card</li>
        <li>not thinner than a credit card</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h2>connections</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>usb-c for power and data</li>
        <li>standard usb midi</li>
        <li>3.5 mm trs midi output</li>
        <li>type a / type b selectable</li>
        <li>web editor uses sysex</li>
        <li>works with computers and hardware that listen to midi</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section id="modes" class="lp-minstore__modes">
    <div><span>faders</span><strong>the normal bit</strong><p>map eight physical positions to eight things you want to hear change.</p></div>
    <div><span>motion</span><strong>the odd bit</strong><p>lift, tilt and rotate. not every patch needs this. the right patch really does.</p></div>
    <div><span>banks</span><strong>the practical bit</strong><p>one setup for the laptop, one for the synth, one for the visual patch, five for later.</p></div>
  </section>

  <section class="lp-minstore__modes">
    <div><span>editor</span><strong>change the map</strong><p>cc numbers, midi channels, banks, fader calibration, midi type. the useful things.</p></div>
    <div><span>learn</span><strong>temporarily quiet</strong><p>midi learn modes stop the accelerometer shouting while you are trying to map a fader.</p></div>
    <div><span>code</span><strong>optional rabbit hole</strong><p>arduino and circuitpython are possible. normal people can ignore this sentence.</p></div>
  </section>

      <h2>in use</h2>
      <p>Put it next to the thing you are controlling. That is the whole design argument. The page should show 8mu on a keyboard, on a small synth, beside a laptop and in one hand. Four places, same object.</p>
