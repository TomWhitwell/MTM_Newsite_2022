      <h2>Small controller, not a large control surface.</h2>
    </div>
    <div>
      <p>8mu does not replace a full-size faderbank. It gives you a few important controls in a small setup. Show it beside common objects so the scale is clear.</p>
      <p>The accelerometer is useful when a gesture is the right control. If it is not useful, turn those channels off.</p>
    </div>
