      <section>
        <p class="sale-kicker">Station</p>
        <h2>Select the file, or scan through a bank.</h2>
        <p>Station is the main control. With long files, it can feel like tuning between sources. With short files, it selects the sound to trigger.</p>
      </section>
      <section>
        <p class="sale-kicker">Bank selection</p>
        <h2>Up to 16 banks on the card.</h2>
        <p>Use banks for different sets of material: voices, drums, field recordings, loops, tones. The four LEDs show bank information.</p>
      </section>
      <section>
        <p class="sale-kicker">Start / Pitch</p>
        <h2>Set the start point, or switch to pitch control.</h2>
        <p>Start can choose where playback begins after Reset. In pitch mode, the same control changes playback speed and pitch.</p>
      </section>
      <section>
        <p class="sale-kicker">Reset</p>
        <h2>Retrigger the sound.</h2>
        <p>Reset can restart playback from the chosen point. It can be played by hand or triggered from another module.</p>
      </section>
      <section>
        <p class="sale-kicker">CV inputs</p>
        <h2>Let the patch choose.</h2>
        <p>Station CV and Start/Pitch CV let the modular system move through files, start points and pitch values.</p>
      </section>
    </div>
  </section>
