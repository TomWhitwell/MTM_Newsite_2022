    <div><h2>Field recordings</h2><p>Put long files in a bank and scan through them with Station. Slow CV can move between locations without a screen.</p></div>
    <div><h2>Loops</h2><p>Use Start and Reset to move a loop’s entry point. Small changes can make a fixed loop sit differently in a patch.</p></div>
    <div><h2>One-shots</h2><p>Trigger short files from Reset. Sequential and random folders can make a bank behave more like a simple sample drum machine.</p></div>
    <div><h2>Live sets</h2><p>Prepare banks for different pieces. Station changes the source quickly; Reset brings the sound back in time.</p></div>

    <div>
      <p class="sale-kicker">What the Remote changes</p>
      <h2>Radio Remote is for people who want to perform the sample.</h2>
      <p>The Remote puts speed, start, end and mode choices on faders. It is useful for tape-style speed changes, ping-pong loops, soft or radio-style station changes, and pulse output behaviour.</p>
      <p>If Radio Music is mainly a source in your rack, the module alone may be enough. If you want to play the sample window and speed with your hands, the Remote makes sense.</p>
    </div>
